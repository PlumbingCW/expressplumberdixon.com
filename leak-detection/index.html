A hidden leak is an emergency when water is already moving

Emergency leak detection in Dixon, CA is for the moment you find water you cannot explain. Look for a growing ceiling stain, a warm or damp wall, a meter dial that keeps turning or a floor that feels soft. Our line is answered 24/7, and a plumber can be scheduled the same day when availability allows.

While you wait, shut off the main water valve if the leak is active. Turn off power to any room where water has reached outlets, and move belongings away from the wet area. If you cannot find the valve, say so on the call.

Meter still running with everything off: how to read it

This is the most common way people learn they have a leak. A few minutes at the meter tells you whether the problem is inside your property or somewhere else.

  1. Turn everything off

    Close every tap, stop the dishwasher and washer, and make sure the ice maker is not filling.

  2. Find the meter

    It is usually in a box near the street, and the box lid lifts with a screwdriver.

  3. Look at the low-flow indicator

    Many meters have a small triangle or dial that spins when any water moves, even a trickle.

  4. Check again after an hour

    If the dial or the numbers moved with all water off, water is leaking somewhere past the meter.

A meter that moves with everything off means a leak on your side of the meter. Then shut the main valve at the house and see whether the meter stops, which tells you if the leak is outside the house or inside it.

Symptoms by location: where leaks hide

Water finds its own path, so the damp spot is rarely above the leak. These are the patterns plumbers see most often.

Ceiling or wall stain

A pipe in the wall or attic is leaking, and the stain is where water finally shows.

Warm spot on the floor

A hot water line under a slab or floor is leaking.

Musty smell or soft flooring

A slow leak in a crawlspace, under a sink or behind a wall.

High bill with no change in habits

A running toilet, an irrigation leak or a line leak is using water.

Sound of water when nothing is on

A pressurized line is leaking inside a wall or under the house.

Crawlspaces, irrigation and yard leaks

A crawlspace leak smells musty and may leave wet soil, white mineral stains on pipes or a sagging insulation batt. It gets worse slowly and is easy to miss until floors soften, so a flashlight check after a few days of high bills is worthwhile.

Irrigation lines are a common leak source in a dry-summer region such as the Sacramento Valley. A wet patch of lawn, a valve box full of water or a sprinkler that never stops can all hide a pressurized leak, and a plumber can test the line separately from the house supply.

A leak in the yard or under a driveway between the meter and the house is a buried line leak. Those are found with listening equipment and a pressure test, and repaired as described on the water line page.

Dixon's water, your bill and what the city can tell you

Source type: City of Dixon water quality report and City of Dixon utility pages.

The City of Dixon supplies water from groundwater wells, and its 2024 report lists system pressure between 52 and 62 pounds per square inch. Constant pressure means a small leak runs all day and night, which is why a pinhole in a line can add a noticeable amount to a monthly bill.

Your utility bill and meter history are the best evidence of a leak. Ask the City of Dixon utility billing office for your recent usage, and compare it with a normal month. A jump with no change in household habits is a reason to run the meter test above.

The city's water operations office handles problems on its side of the meter. If you are not sure where the city's responsibility ends and yours begins at your address, ask them before you pay for repairs.

Pipe rules shape where a leak can hide. The City of Dixon's handout cites section 604.1 of the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), which does not allow PVC or polyethylene for water supply in the situations it covers.
