Backup that a snake could not fix: what to do now

Emergency hydro jetting in Dixon, CA is available through our 24/7 line when a backup cannot wait. Stop running water, tell the dispatcher that a snake already failed or that the drain clogs again every few weeks, and a licensed plumber can come the same day when availability allows.

Hydro jetting is drain cleaning with a high-pressure water hose. A jetting nozzle sends water backward along the pipe wall, which scours grease, scale and soap film loose and flushes it down the line, where a snake would only open a channel through the middle.

A snake got stuck or the clog came back: what that tells a plumber

Callers often describe the same two stories. One is a snake that stopped partway down the line and would not go farther. The other is a drain that works for a short while and then clogs again. Both point at the condition of the pipe, not just the blockage.

The snake stops at the same spot

Something solid, like a root mass or a joint that has shifted, may be in the way.

Water drains, then slows again

A narrowed pipe wall holds buildup that a snake never removes.

Greasy buildup from a kitchen line

Cooled grease coats the pipe and shrinks the opening a little more each week.

Roots in the line

Fine root hairs reach into joints and come back unless they are cut cleanly.

A camera run before jetting shows which of these is happening. Our sewer camera inspection page explains what you get from that step.

Snaking versus hydro jetting for a repeat clog

Both methods have a place. The question is whether you need a clear opening today or a clean pipe for the long run.

A snake or cable alone

  • Opens a channel quickly for a fresh clog.
  • Leaves a coating of grease or scale on the pipe wall.
  • Can tear through soft roots but may not remove them.
  • Often needs repeating when the line is greasy.

Hydro jetting

  • Scours the full circumference of the pipe.
  • Flushes debris down the line instead of leaving it.
  • Cuts fine roots and clears scale at the joints.
  • Pairs well with a camera check before and after.

Drain cleaning with a snake is still the right first call for many clogs, and our drain cleaning page explains how a plumber chooses.

When hydro jetting is the wrong tool

Jetting cleans pipes that are intact. It is not the answer for a pipe that is cracked, collapsed or pulled apart at a joint, because high-pressure water can push into the gap and make the ground around the pipe worse.

That is why a camera view comes first on an older line or a line with a history of problems. If the camera shows a break, the job becomes a repair, which is covered on our sewer line repair page.

What scale and hard water do to Dixon pipes

Source type: City of Dixon water quality report.

The City of Dixon reports total hardness in its groundwater averaging 163 parts per million for 2024, with readings between 130 and 190. Hard water leaves mineral scale where it is heated or evaporates, and it combines with soap to form scum on drain walls.

In a kitchen or laundry line, that scale roughens the pipe wall and gives grease something to cling to. Jetting removes both layers in one pass, which is the main reason a repeat clog in a hard-water town often responds better to jetting than to a second snaking.

Jetting usually enters the line through a cleanout. The California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), requires cleanouts at set points on a sewer line, and the City of Dixon's handout cites section 719.1 for the spacing.

Pipe size and lengthA longer or larger line takes more time and more water.
