A water service line leak is an emergency when it floods the yard or kills pressure

Emergency water line repair in Dixon, CA covers the pipe that carries city water from the meter to your house. When it leaks, you may see a soggy patch in the yard or a driveway that stays damp, or you may only notice that the bill climbs and the pressure drops.

Our line is answered 24/7. If water is surfacing, shut off the main valve at your house. If the leak is on the city side of the meter, the City of Dixon lists an after-hours water emergency line at (707) 676-3005.

How a failing water line shows itself

Water lines fail slowly and then suddenly. These are the cues worth noticing.

A meter that runs with everything off

Water is leaking past the meter, often in the buried line.

A wet or sunken patch between the meter and the house

The soil is saturated by a leak below.

Low or fading pressure

Leaks and corroded pipe both reduce what reaches the fixtures.

Brown or discolored water

Corrosion inside an older line sheds rust into the supply.

Finding the exact spot is a detection job first. Our leak detection page explains how the leak is pinpointed before digging.

What Dixon requires on a water line repair

Source type: City of Dixon Building Division water line handout.

The City of Dixon's water line repair and replacement handout lists the rules for the work. Type L copper is expected below the building, copper under a slab has no joints, and PVC and polyethylene are not allowed for the supply. Plastic that is allowed needs a tracer wire so it can be found again, and the trench needs at least 12 inches of cover.

The finished line is pressure tested for 15 minutes at working pressure. If the job touches the city connection, a sidewalk or the street, an encroachment permit applies, and that is its own approval from the city.

A repair that replaces the line usually involves a permit and an inspection. Ask the plumber who pulls the permit and whether the quote includes it.

Section 604.1 of the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), as cited in the City of Dixon's handout, is the rule behind the pipe choice. It also sets the tracer wire and cover depth the handout lists.

Low water pressure: when the line is the cause

The City of Dixon reports system pressure between 52 and 62 pounds per square inch, so a house with very low pressure has something wrong on its own side of the meter. A partly closed valve, a failing regulator, buildup inside old galvanized pipe or a leak can all be responsible.

A pressure gauge on a hose bib gives you a number to share with the plumber. Low pressure on hot only points at the water heater, while low pressure everywhere points at the line, the valve or the regulator.
