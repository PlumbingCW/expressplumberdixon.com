Emergency: no hot water or a leaking tank in Dixon

Emergency water heater repair in Dixon, CA covers the two calls that cannot wait: a unit that has stopped making hot water, and a tank that is leaking. Our line is answered 24/7, and a licensed plumber can come the same day when availability allows.

If you see water around the tank, shut off the cold supply valve above it and cut power at the breaker or turn the gas control to off. Do not touch wet wiring. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.

What the symptom tells a plumber

Most water heater problems announce themselves in one of a few ways. Knowing which one you have makes the call faster and the diagnosis more accurate.

No hot water at all

A gas unit may have lost its pilot or thermocouple, and an electric unit may have a failed element, thermostat or tripped breaker.

Water, but not hot enough

A worn lower element, a thermostat set too low or sediment on the bottom of the tank can all cut capacity.

Water at a fitting or valve

A dripping connection or relief valve can often be repaired before the tank is damaged.

Water from the tank body

A leak from the shell means the tank has corroded and needs replacement.

Rumbling, popping or discolored water

Sediment, scale or a depleted anode rod is usually behind it.

If the leak is from the tank body, our water heater replacement page explains the next step.

Gas, electric and hybrid units: what usually fails

Each type has its own common failures. This table covers the parts a plumber checks first.

Unit typeCommon failureWhat gets checkedUsual outcome
Gas tankPilot or thermocouple, gas control valve, ventingFlame, draft and gas connectionRepair of the failed part, or replacement on an old tank.
Electric tankHeating element, thermostat, tripped breakerPower supply and element resistancePart replacement is common and quick.
Hybrid or heat pumpAirflow, condensate drain, control or sensor faultsFilter, condensate line, error codesRepair of the fault, or service call to the manufacturer for sealed parts.

Hybrid and heat pump units are a growing share of the market. They need clear airflow and a free-draining condensate line, so cleaning and drain checks fix many service calls.

What you can check yourself and what to leave alone

A few checks cost nothing and help the plumber. Anything involving gas or live wiring belongs to a licensed professional.

Safe to check

  • Is the breaker on, or is the gas control set to on.
  • Is the cold water shutoff valve fully open.
  • Is the thermostat set to a sensible temperature.
  • Is there water on the floor, and where is it coming from.

Leave to a plumber

  • Relighting a pilot on an older unit that will not hold a flame.
  • Replacing elements, thermostats or gas control valves.
  • Anything involving the relief valve discharge.
  • A tank that is leaking from the body.

Hard water, sediment and Dixon water heaters

Source type: City of Dixon water quality report.

The City of Dixon's 2024 water quality report lists total hardness averaging 163 parts per million, about 9.5 grains per gallon. Hard water leaves scale on heating elements and on the bottom of the tank, where it insulates the water from the burner or the lower element.

That is why many water heater calls in a hard-water area involve a rumbling tank, longer recovery times or a failed lower element. A flush now and then clears loose sediment, and a plumber checking the anode rod can tell you whether the tank still has protection left.

If scale keeps returning, a softener changes the water before it reaches the heater. Our water softener page explains how that works in Dixon.

A repair visit should leave the install compliant. The City of Dixon's handout cites section 504.6 of the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) for a hard-piped relief line and section 507.2 for seismic straps.
