Plumbing Services in Dixon, CA

Twenty plumbing services, one 24/7 line. Choose the problem you have to see the signs, the steps and the questions to ask.

Each page below explains one kind of plumbing problem in plain language. You will find what to do before the plumber arrives, what affects the price and what Dixon's permit rules say. Many are emergencies, and the line is open all day and night.

Not sure which page fits? Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. The dispatcher will point you to the right service.

Water Heater Replacement

A failed tank cannot wait for a long lead time. Call the 24/7 line for a replacement plan, with same-day appointments when available.

Emergency Drain Cleaning

Slow sinks, backed-up tubs and gurgling toilets get a live answer on our 24/7 line, with same-day appointments when available and a clear price before work starts.

Water Heater Repair

Cold showers and puddles under the tank get a real answer at any hour. The 24/7 line can schedule a repair visit the same day when available.

Water Heater Installation

A planned install lets you choose the unit, the location and the fuel before anything fails. Call to talk through your options and schedule the work.

Tankless Water Heater

Tankless water heaters heat water on demand and last longer than tanks, but they need the right gas line, vent and regular descaling.

Hydro Jetting

When a snake only buys a few weeks, hydro jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Call the 24/7 line to talk through whether it fits your clog.

Leak Detection

Most leak calls start with a symptom, not a diagnosis. Tell us what you see and the 24/7 line can send a plumber to find the source.

Slab Leak Repair

A leak under the concrete does not announce itself. If you see the signs, call the 24/7 line and get a plumber who can confirm it.

Sewer Line Repair

When cleaning shows a cracked or collapsed pipe, the next step is a repair plan. Our 24/7 line gets the right plumber to look at the line.

Sewer Camera Inspection

A sewer camera inspection shows what is inside the pipe, so repair and cleaning decisions rest on a picture instead of a guess.

Sewer Line Cleaning

A backed-up main line is a same-day problem. Call the 24/7 line, stop using water and find your cleanout while a plumber is scheduled.

Burst Pipe Repair

A burst pipe floods a house in minutes. Shut off the water, then call the 24/7 line for a same-day repair when available.

Water Line Repair

The water line from the meter to your house is out of sight and out of mind until it fails. Call the 24/7 line when it does.

Toilet Repair and Installation

An overflowing toilet or a leak at the base is a same-day problem. Call the 24/7 line, shut off the supply valve and keep the water out of the floor.

Faucet and Sink Repair

A faucet that will not shut off, or a supply line spraying under a sink, cannot wait. Call the 24/7 line and shut the supply valve.

Garbage Disposal Repair

A disposal that leaks into the cabinet or backs up the sink needs attention today. Call the 24/7 line and stop using the sink.

Repiping

When the same supply lines keep failing, replacing them is cheaper than chasing leaks. Call to book a same-day appointment when available.

Plumbing Installation

Kitchens, baths, laundry rooms and additions all start with rough plumbing done right. Call to schedule, with same-day appointments when available.

Commercial Plumbing

When a restaurant, shop or office loses water or drains, every hour costs money. Call the 24/7 line, close the main valve and keep people out of wet areas.

Water Softener Installation

Dixon water is hard, and scale shows up in heaters, fixtures and glassware. Call to talk through whether a softener makes sense for your home.
