When a failed water heater becomes an emergency

Emergency water heater replacement in Dixon, CA applies when a tank is leaking from the body, has no hot water and cannot be repaired, or is flooding a closet or garage. Our line is answered 24/7, and a licensed plumber can be scheduled the same day when availability allows.

Before anyone arrives, shut off the cold water supply valve at the top of the tank and turn off the power or the gas control. Water leaking from the tank body means the steel has corroded through, and no repair fixes that.

Repair or replace: five signs the tank is finished

Many calls begin with the same question. Most storage tanks last 10 to 15 years according to the U.S. Department of Energy, so age is the first clue, and these are the others.

Water pooling at the base

A leak from the tank body is a replacement, not a repair.

Rusty hot water

Rust-colored water from hot taps only often means the tank has begun to corrode inside.

Age near or past 10 to 15 years

Repairs cost more as the tank gets older and other parts follow the first failure.

Rumbling or popping

Sediment hardens at the bottom of the tank and the heater struggles to heat through it.

Repeated repairs

Several service calls in a year are a sign the money is better spent on a new unit.

If the tank is not leaking and the problem is no hot water or a drip at a fitting, a repair may be enough. See our water heater repair page before deciding.

Dixon's permit rules for water heater changeouts

Source type: City of Dixon Building Division water heater handout.

The City of Dixon offers an instant permit for a water heater replacement, but only for a direct replacement of like kind, function and capacity. A gas tank swapped for a tankless or heat pump unit is not eligible for the instant permit, and neither is any job that adds new gas piping or electrical work.

The handout lists what the finished job has to show. The tank needs earthquake strapping, with two straps in the upper and lower thirds and 4 inches of clearance to the controls. The relief valve must discharge through hard pipe to the outside, between 6 and 24 inches above grade.

A secondary pan with a three-quarter inch drain is called for where a leak could cause damage. Gas units need a new listed flexible connector no longer than 3 feet, an accessible shutoff and a sediment trap. Garage heaters need the burner at least 18 inches above the floor, and the job ends with a final inspection and a smoke and carbon monoxide check.

The permit requires a contractor with a California contractor license and a City of Dixon business license. Ask to see both before you hand over a deposit.

What a replacement day looks like

A like-for-like swap is usually a half-day job once the new unit is on hand. Here is the order of work.

  1. Shut down and drain

    Water, power or gas are shut off and the old tank is drained.

  2. Remove the old unit

    Connections are disconnected and the tank is carried out for disposal.

  3. Set and connect

    The new heater is placed on a pan, strapped, and piped with a new relief valve and discharge line.

  4. Test and inspect

    The unit is filled, fired or powered, checked for leaks and combustion, and set up for the inspection.

What changes the price of a replacement

Replacement pricing is a sum of real parts and labor, not a flat figure. These factors decide the total.
