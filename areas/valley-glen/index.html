What two decades of use does to a plumbing system

A house finished between 2003 and 2005 is now about 21 to 23 years old. Many parts in a house that age are on a second life or overdue for one.

The original water heater

A storage tank past 10 to 15 years can leak at the base with little warning. Rusty hot water and popping sounds are early signs.

Scale on fixtures

Hard water leaves white crust on aerators and showerheads and cuts the flow. Cleaning or replacing the part often restores pressure.

Stuck shutoff valves

Valves under sinks and toilets that have not moved in years can seize or weep when turned. Test them before an emergency.

Worn toilet parts

A flapper or fill valve that runs on its own wastes water and raises the bill. These parts wear out in every home.

A kitchen drain that keeps slowing

Grease and food residue build up over the years. A sink that clears briefly and slows again points to buildup in the line.

A leaking tank is a water heater repair or replacement call, and fixture trouble is covered on our faucet repair page.

Dixon water inside a 2000s house

Dixon pumps groundwater only. The city reports an average hardness of 163 parts per million, with readings between 130 and 190. That is hard water on the U.S. Geological Survey scale.

Scale from that water settles on heating elements, shower valves and aerators. The U.S. Department of Energy advises flushing a heater in hard water, which matters for a tank already past its tenth year.

A leaking water heater in Valley Glen: first steps

Water at the base of the tank is a common sign that a heater has reached the end of its life. These steps keep the damage small until a plumber arrives.

  1. Shut off the power or gas

    Switch off the breaker, or set a gas unit to pilot.

  2. Close the cold supply valve

    It sits on the pipe above the tank and turns clockwise.

  3. Move items away from the floor

    Garage storage and boxes soak up water fast.

  4. Take photos of the tank label and the leak

    The label shows the age, and photos help any insurance claim.

  5. Call and give the tank size and fuel type

    That lets the plumber bring the right parts.

If you smell natural gas, leave the home and call 9-1-1 first.
