Sewer lateral rules a plumber must follow in Dixon

Source type: City of Dixon sewer lateral permit handout.

The city handout cites section 719.1 of the California Plumbing Code for cleanouts. They go at the junction of the drain and sewer, at the property line, at the end of the line, at turns over 135 degrees and every 100 feet.

Section 710.1 calls for a backwater valve when fixtures sit below the cover of the next upstream manhole. Trenchless and liner repairs need a camera test, and a standard line is tested for 15 minutes under a 10 foot head of water or 5 psi of air.

Clearing a stoppage or stopping a leak is exempt from permits, but replacing a concealed pipe with new pipe needs one.

The code behind these rules is the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).
