Dixon plumbing

What Dixon water and housing mean for your pipes

Dixon takes its water from deep groundwater wells. The City of Dixon's 2024 report lists total hardness averaging 163 parts per million, which the U.S. Geological Survey scale classes as hard and which is enough to leave scale in water heaters, faucets and shower valves. System pressure runs between 52 and 62 pounds per square inch, and that steady push keeps any small leak flowing day and night.

That is why a water heater that is flushed and a leak that is caught early matter here. Our water heater replacement and leak detection pages explain what to do before a small problem turns into a flood.

The town itself is older than most of its neighbors. Dixon was incorporated in 1878, and a fire in 1883 destroyed much of the downtown. Rebuilding brought brick buildings, so businesses near the center may have older lines that need a closer look. Our commercial plumbing page covers what building owners should know.

Emergency plumber

What a good emergency plumber call looks like

An emergency plumber should answer the phone, ask where the water is and tell you what to do while you wait. You should hear the price before the work starts, and the plumber should explain what happened in plain words.

If water is pouring, close the main water valve first. If a drain has backed up, stop running water in the house. If you smell natural gas, leave the home and call 9-1-1 before you call a plumber. These steps keep damage small, and our burst pipe repair page has a longer checklist.

California also protects you when you hire. A contractor license is required for work of $1,000 or more in labor and materials, and you can check a license at cslb.ca.gov. A down payment is capped at 10 percent of the price or $1,000, whichever is less.
